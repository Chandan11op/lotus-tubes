## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision, hygienic industrial visual language tailored for advanced sanitary fluid architecture, pharmaceutical bioprocessing, and ultra-clean dairy technologies. The brand personality blends Swiss-grade mechanical discipline with sleek high-tech luxury: clinical, sterile, ultra-durable, and uncompromisingly precise. 

The aesthetic synthesizes **Precision Industrial Minimalism** with **Crystalline Glassmorphism**. Dark polished obsidian surfaces serve as cleanroom-grade backdrops, while polished chrome borders, machined graphite modules, and cold liquid-metal lusters provide tactile materiality. Precision cyan and vivid magenta act as laser-guided diagnostic accents, symbolizing thermal control, flow velocity, sterile assurance, and automated fluid routing. The intended emotional impact is total engineering confidence, absolute contamination control, and forward-looking industrial mastery.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in ultra-deep obsidian foundations, layered with cold-worked austenitic stainless steel metallics, and energized by spectral cleanroom diagnostics:

- **Base Canvas & Graphites**:
  - `Canvas Dark`: `#030810` (Deep cleanroom obsidian, infinite depth).
  - `Surface Graphite`: `#0B132B` / `#0F172A` (Durable structural substrate for cards and tooling panels).
  - `Surface Elevated`: `#1E293B` (Machined surface elevation with 60% opacity).
- **Metallic Chrome & Steel**:
  - `Steel Ultra-Light`: `#F8FAFC` (Liquid chrome reflections & pure white technical headings).
  - `Steel Polished`: `#E2E8F0` (Electropolished Ra < 0.4µm tube finishes & primary borders).
  - `Steel Satin`: `#CBD5E1` (Secondary metadata, structural ribs, and technical schematics).
  - `Steel Muted`: `#64748B` to `#94A3B8` (Passive pipe runs, dimensional lines, deactivated states).
- **Precision Flow Accents**:
  - `Primary Cyan`: `#00F0FF` (Cryogenic flow, laminar purity, active sensors, and positive feedback).
  - `Secondary Magenta`: `#FF007A` (Thermal process lines, critical valve warnings, seal integrity, and active flow toggles).

Gradient fills replicate physical tube specular highlights: use linear gradients angled at `135deg` from `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.2)` down to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` across metallic borders.

## Typography

The type ecosystem balances monumental industrial engineering with microscopic metallurgical telemetry:

- **Display & Headings (`Space Grotesk`)**: Geometric, sharp, and confident. Headings reflect clean mechanical blueprints and architectural manifold schedules. Always set pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or metallic cold silver (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Body & Continuous Reading (`Geist`)**: Neutral, hyper-legible, and anti-glare for industrial catalog browsing and technical documentation. Neutral high-contrast grey tints (`#CBD5E1`) prevent eye fatigue on dark interfaces.
- **Data, Dimensions & Specifications (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Applied to all ASME BPE dimensions, surface finish tolerances (Ra), metallurgy heat numbers, pressure thresholds, and valve serialization. Always styled in uppercase for micro badges and schematic pins.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a rigid, fluid 12-column engineering grid overlaying a subtle 24px micro-grid blueprint coordinate pattern (composed of `#00F0FF` at 3% opacity).

- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Mobile` (< 768px): 4-column layout, edge margins set to `margin-sm` (1rem), components collapse linearly into single vertical process conduits.
  - `Tablet` (768px – 1024px): 8-column layout, edge margins set to `margin` (2rem), gutter at `gutter-sm`.
  - `Desktop` (1025px+): 12-column dynamic layout with structural side-rails, edge margins up to `margin-lg` (4rem).
- **Rhythm & Structure**: 
  Component spacing follows an exact 4px/8px modular pitch mimicking precision machining tolerances. Density is strictly high-efficiency: informational headers, engineering schematics, and tolerance parameter tables minimize unnecessary vertical negative space in favor of compact, modular data clusters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects fuzzy generic dropshadows in favor of cold, machined optical refraction:

1. **Cleanroom Substrate (Ground Zero)**: Pure `#030810` base canvas accompanied by an ultra-faint vector micro-grid that renders components as physical blueprints.
2. **Glassmorphic Stainless Steel Panels (Level 1)**: Translucent graphite (`rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.65)`) with an integrated 16px backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`). Bound by a 1px metallic hairline border: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.3) 0%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05) 50%, rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15) 100%)`.
3. **Electropolished Hover & Active Tiers (Level 2)**: Elevation is achieved via directional laser rim lighting and neon dispersion. Active cards and focused modules emit a concentrated directional glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -4px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25), inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`.
4. **Modal Dialogs & Critical Diagnostics (Level 3)**: Deep graphite panels bounded by high-contrast polished chrome edging, layered over an obsidian blackout scrim with an inner diagnostic magenta hazard-line glow: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 30px -5px rgba(255, 0, 122, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts **Level 1 (Soft)** geometry. Radii are kept precise, clipped, and clinical (4px for inputs, badges, and buttons; 8px for structural glass panels; 12px maximum for master system containers). 

This restrained corner radius replicates CNC-machined stainless steel billets, cold-rolled ferrules, and pharmaceutical valve blocks that have had burrs safely chamfered without losing industrial structural rigidity. Full circular radii are strictly restricted to pipe flow cross-section diagrams, round status indicators, and sanitary clamp gaskets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Precision Hydro-Action)**: Solid high-velocity Cyan background (`#00F0FF`) with pure obsidian text (`#030810`), 4px border radius, typed in `Space Grotesk` medium. Hover triggers a dual metallic reflection sweep (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(255,255,255,0.4), transparent)`) and an external glow (`0 0 16px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.5)`).
- **Secondary (Machined Steel)**: Translucent graphite body (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.05)`), 1px solid polished chrome border (`#94A3B8`), pure white text. Hover transitions border to `#00F0FF` with a subtle interior fill brightened to `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.12)`.
- **Destructive / High-Temp Stop**: High-visibility vivid Magenta (`#FF007A`) border and accent glow with technical monospaced label.

### Chips & Spec Badges
- Constructed with `JetBrains Mono` at `label-code-micro`.
- Standard alloy spec chips (e.g., `316L S.S.`, `DIN 11850`, `Ra < 0.38µm`, `EP-POLISHED`) feature deep obsidian fills, 1px `#334155` border, and text tinted in `#E2E8F0`. Active filter states illuminate with cyan borders and matching micro-dots.

### Lists & Technical Spec Tables
- Precision matrix design with zero alternating zebra striping; separation relies on 1px horizontal metallic hairline dividers (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.08)`).
- Row hover triggers an instantaneous left-aligned cyan or magenta flow marker line (2px wide) and a subtle gradient backlight illumination across the entire row.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Crisp 4px squared forms for checkboxes; concentric precision rings for radio buttons.
- Unchecked: 1px border of `#64748B` over `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)`.
- Checked: Electric cyan border and neon-cyan core fill, accompanied by a sharp, geometric checkmark in obsidian.

### Input Fields & CAD Selectors
- Background: Polished dark graphite (`rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.8)`).
- Border: 1px cold stainless steel (`#334155`).
- Typography: `Geist` for entered values, with engineering units (e.g., `mm`, `OD`, `bar`, `Ra`) locked on the right in `JetBrains Mono` styled in `#64748B`.
- Focus State: Smooth glow transition to `#00F0FF` border with a 0.2s cubic ease-out.

### Cards & Modular Manifolds
- Encapsulated within 1px metallic chrome border glassmorphic containers. 
- Integrated header bars displaying technical component identifiers (e.g., `FERRULE-TC-DIN32676`) accompanied by micro CAD wireframe icons and status telemetry beacons.

### Specialized Components
- **Flow Directionals & Valve Toggles**: Animated fluid SVG pulse indicators that track flow speed (cyan for intake/laminar flow, magenta for CIP/SIP sterilization and high-heat bypass).
- **Surface Finish Dial**: Visual gauge rendering specular metallic roughness from 0.8µm down to 0.2µm Mirror Electropolished finishes.
