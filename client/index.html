Quality Assurance & Documentation

Quality control is integral to every tube, fitting, and valve distributed by Lotus Tubes. Every batch undergoes comprehensive mechanical, dimensional, and surface finish inspection before dispatch.

  • EN 10204 3.1 Mill Test Certificates (MTC): Full chemical composition (C, Cr, Ni, Mo) and mechanical strength verification.
  • Surface Roughness Testing: Calibrated profilometer readings ensuring internal finish compliance (Ra ≤ 0.4 µm for electropolished, Ra ≤ 0.8 µm for mechanically polished).
  • Hydrostatic & Eddy Current Testing: 100% non-destructive pressure and flaw detection testing on all sanitary tubing.
  • Material Traceability: Laser-etched heat numbers, material grade (SS 304 / SS 316L), and standard markings on each component.
